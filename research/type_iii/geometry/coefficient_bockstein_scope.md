# The coefficient exact sequence and its cohomology maps

Fix distinct primes `p` and `ell`. Write

\[
\Lambda_n=(\mathbf Z/\ell^{n+1}\mathbf Z)[T]/\Phi_p(T),
\qquad \Lambda=\varprojlim_n\Lambda_n,
\]

with the original projections `π_n` and reductions `ρ_nm`, for `m ≤ n`.
The limit ring is finite free over `Z_ell`. Consequently multiplication
by any power of `ell` is injective on it. The original projection has
kernel `(ell^(n+1))`, and the original finite reduction has kernel
generated by `ell^(m+1)`. These assertions concern the rings already
constructed in the development; no choice of a field factor is made.

## The sheaf short exact sequence

For a phase `f` on the original characteristic-`p` affine scheme, let
`L_Λ` and `L_n` be its constructed Artin–Schreier character sheaves.
There is a proved short exact sequence of `Λ`-module sheaves

\[
0\longrightarrow\mathcal L_\Lambda
\xrightarrow{\ell^{n+1}}\mathcal L_\Lambda
\xrightarrow{\pi_n}\operatorname{Res}_{\pi_n}\mathcal L_n
\longrightarrow0.
\tag{1}
\]

The second arrow is the original coefficient map. At a geometric
point, the projected identity section identifies that map with the
literal ring projection. Its kernel and surjectivity, together with
regularity of `ell`, therefore give stalkwise short exactness. The
proved conservative family of geometric points detects exactness of
the original sheaf sequence.

The sources are
[`TypeIIITorsionCoefficientExactness.lean`](../../../formal/TypeIIITorsionCoefficientExactness.lean),
[`TypeIIIArtinSchreierCoefficientGenerator.lean`](../../../formal/TypeIIIArtinSchreierCoefficientGenerator.lean),
and [`TypeIIIArtinSchreierBockstein.lean`](../../../formal/TypeIIIArtinSchreierBockstein.lean).

## Cohomology of the fixed Kloosterman family

Let `j` be the original open immersion of the phase family into its
projective-plane compactification, and `q` its proper projection to the
parameter line. Put

\[
H^d=R^dq_*(j_!\mathcal L_\Lambda),\qquad
T_n^d=\operatorname{Res}_{\pi_n}
       R^dq_*(j_!\mathcal L_n).
\tag{2}
\]

These are sheaves on the parameter line. In `T_n^d`, the derived image
is computed in the original finite coefficient category. The proved
coefficient comparison identifies it with the corresponding term of
the original tower computed over `Λ`.

The original `j_!` is exact. Applying it to (1), then using the proved
long exact sequence for the original right derived functors, gives

\[
\cdots\longrightarrow H^d\xrightarrow{\ell^{n+1}}H^d
\xrightarrow{r_n^d}T_n^d\xrightarrow{\delta_n^d}H^{d+1}
\xrightarrow{\ell^{n+1}}H^{d+1}\longrightarrow\cdots.
\tag{3}
\]

All three consecutive triples displayed here are exact. The reduction
`r_n^d` is the original derived reduction followed by the inverse of the
original finite coefficient comparison. The connecting map `δ_n^d`
is the original comparison followed by the canonical derived connecting
map. Thus the exact sequence uses the independently defined finite
cohomology objects and their original maps.

See [`TypeIIIKloostermanBockstein.lean`](../../../formal/TypeIIIKloostermanBockstein.lean)
and [`TypeIIIKloostermanFiniteBockstein.lean`](../../../formal/TypeIIIKloostermanFiniteBockstein.lean).

## Dependence on the coefficient level

The morphism from (1) at level `n` to (1) at level `m ≤ n` has
components

\[
(\ell^{n-m}\mathrm{id},\ \mathrm{id},\ \rho_{nm}).
\tag{4}
\]

The first square commutes because
`ell^(n-m) ell^(m+1) = ell^(n+1)`; the second is the original projection
compatibility. The original short-complex transitions satisfy identity
and composition. Their derived connecting maps satisfy the canonical
naturality square, with multiplication by `ell^(n-m)` on the next
ordinary cohomology term. These assertions are proved in
[`TypeIIIArtinSchreierBocksteinTransitions.lean`](../../../formal/TypeIIIArtinSchreierBocksteinTransitions.lean)
and [`TypeIIIKloostermanBocksteinTransitions.lean`](../../../formal/TypeIIIKloostermanBocksteinTransitions.lean).

For the independently defined finite transition `t_nm^d : T_n^d → T_m^d`,
the original finite coefficient comparisons give

\[
t_{nm}^d r_n^d=r_m^d,\qquad
\delta_m^d t_{nm}^d=\ell^{n-m}\delta_n^d.
\tag{5}
\]

Let `P(H^d)` denote the system with object `H^d` at every level and
transition `ell^(n-m)`, and let `const(H^d)` have identity transitions.
The maps in (3) then form

\[
P(H^d)\longrightarrow\operatorname{const}(H^d)
\longrightarrow (T_n^d)_n\longrightarrow P(H^{d+1})
\longrightarrow\operatorname{const}(H^{d+1}).
\tag{6}
\]

All three consecutive triples in (6) are proved exact in the category
of sheaf-valued inverse systems. Evaluation at each level detects
exactness and recovers the original maps in (3). This assembly, including
(5), is in
[`TypeIIIKloostermanBocksteinSystem.lean`](../../../formal/TypeIIIKloostermanBocksteinSystem.lean).
It takes no inverse limit.

## Scope of the conclusion

The terms `H^d` in (2) are ordinary derived images of a sheaf with
discrete `Λ` coefficients. At the level of sheaves and geometric stalks,
sequence (3) identifies the torsion obstruction to lifting a finite
coefficient class to these ordinary terms. It does not identify them
with adic cohomology.

The construction has not commuted a derived image with an inverse
limit, proved a Mittag–Leffler assertion for the cohomology tower, or
established the required adic finiteness and trace comparisons. Nor
does it estimate the Type III Fourier coefficient. Those remain
separate proof requirements, recorded in the
[progress map](../../../docs/type-iii.md).
